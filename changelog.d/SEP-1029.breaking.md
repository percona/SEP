`POST /api/oauth/refresh` now requires an `HttpOnly` `refreshToken` cookie scoped to `/api/oauth` instead of a body field; response shape is slimmed to `{access_token, expires_in}` (no `refresh_token`, `id_token`, `token_type`, or `scope`). Pair with the new `POST /api/oauth/login` and `POST /api/oauth/logout`.
