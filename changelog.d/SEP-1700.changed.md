Task-log anonymization now caps how many raw bytes may be withheld while awaiting a line terminator (default 1 MiB). An un-terminated step that emits a huge partial line no longer stalls log persistence or the live viewer, and no longer drives quadratic Nomad re-fetching; once the ceiling is exceeded the buffer is flushed and a warning names the allocation, step, and log type. Existing deployments pick up the default with no configuration change; the ceiling is the advanced hot override TASKS.NOMAD.log_anonymization_max_withheld_bytes (NOMAD__log_anonymization_max_withheld_bytes), so an override lands on the next log sync or live-stream frame without a deploy.
