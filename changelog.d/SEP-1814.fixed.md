A PMM Extensions web process or Celery worker child that starts while a `LOGGING` override already exists now applies it at boot instead of logging at the YAML/env level until the override is next changed. This covers the two processes that own `Settings` override rows; standalone Tasks and Inventory processes do not wire the global `Settings` proxy, so a stored `LOGGING` override remains invisible to them at boot and at runtime alike, and they keep logging at their own YAML/env level.
