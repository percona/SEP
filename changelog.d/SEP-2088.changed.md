The Support diagnostics category browser now reaches 36 of the 89 builtin scripts, up from 15; the remaining 53 are deliberately search-only and stay reachable from the Snippets catalog.
The ProxySQL and HAProxy category roots are populated for the first time — the two ProxySQL scripts now declare `service_type: proxysql`, and the two HAProxy scripts now declare categories — while the ProxySQL alerts continue to appear under MySQL in Alert Troubleshooting.
The snippet frontmatter key declaring browser categories is now `diagnostic_categories:` rather than `atw:`, so a locally authored script still declaring `atw:` no longer appears in the browser and has to be renamed.
