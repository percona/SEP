Celery prefork worker children now refresh DB-backed settings overrides at task boundaries (task_prerun) rather than on a background timer; web processes keep the periodic refresh loop. An override therefore takes effect on the next due task a child runs after SETTINGS_OVERRIDE.REFRESH_INTERVAL elapses — not mid-task, and not while a child sits idle — so work already in flight or enqueued before that boundary may still run against the previous snapshot.
