Added the OpenManager Inventory app, which sweeps the MongoDB estate over Nomad every ten minutes by default and serves the hosts, services and run history it collects under `/api/apps/om_inventory/`, configured through the new `SEP.OM_INVENTORY` settings block or the app's own `/config` endpoint. It is activated in the shipped side-car, and the half that consumes it ships in pmm-managed, so a PMM built without `managed/services/om` reaches the endpoints with nothing reading them.
