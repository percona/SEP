The MySQL Restores create form now opens its backup-specific fields with Destination Database Service, ahead of the restore method, and requires it for a Mydumper restore: a Mydumper body naming no service is rejected at body validation with a message naming the field, instead of failing later inside the inventory lookup. XtraBackup and Binlog restores still accept no destination service. The field's helper text now says it populates Backup Source and the Mydumper target database.
