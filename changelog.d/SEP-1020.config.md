New `TASKS__HEALTH_CHECK_INTERVAL` setting (default 15 minutes) controls how often the Tasks Celery worker probes each executor host, `TASKS__NOMAD__HEALTH_CHECK_TIMEOUT` (default 30 seconds) bounds how long the Nomad probe waits per host, and `TASKS__NOMAD__HEALTH_CHECK_POLL_INTERVAL` (default 1 second) is how often the probe polls Nomad for job termination within that budget
