The PMM side-car's baked delivery plan now declares a connectivity probe and a connection-details read, so a side-car whose ServiceNow credentials are supplied through `DIAGNOSTICS_DELIVERY_INPUTS` reports whether the receiver is reachable instead of `probe_undeclared`, and names the account, key and expiry that credential carries instead of `undeclared`; a side-car with no credentials supplied, and every standalone deployment, is unaffected.
