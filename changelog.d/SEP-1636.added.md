Encrypt Mydumper and Binlog backups with xbcrypt/AES-256 and restore `.xbcrypt` backups with the configured AES-256 key file. Backup and restore forms now offer AES-256 and AES-256 + GPG for those engines (previously XtraBackup-only); AES-256 + GPG reports as dual but applies AES-256 only, matching XtraBackup. Existing GPG-only encryption is unchanged.
