Renamed the side-car's contracts, stored names and user-facing text from SEP to PMM Extensions: settings use the `EXTENSIONS__` environment prefix and `EXTENSIONS` YAML section, deployment inputs and the build argument are `EXTENSIONS_*`, the side-car is served under `/extensions/` with its API at `/api/extensions/`, its Grafana service account is `pmm-extensions`, annotations and alerting objects are named PMM Extensions, and its state volume is `pmm-extensions-state` mounted at `/home/extensions/state`. The old names are not accepted as aliases.
