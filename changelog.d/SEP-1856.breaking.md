Manual inventory authoring is removed: POST /api/apps/inventory/{entity}/, PUT and DELETE /api/apps/inventory/{entity}/{item_id} no longer exist, and the four inventory entity schemas no longer declare create forms or row actions. PMM is the source of truth for nodes, services, schemas and tables; the inventory service's own write routes under /api/inventory/ are unchanged, so syncer writes keep working. Reads, sync and connectivity checks are unaffected.
